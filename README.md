# Mini-workshops at TPAC 2026

All proposals for these mini-workshops are to be submitted to, by **30 September**, as an [issue](https://github.com/w3c/tpac2026-miniworkshops), using the corresponding issue template.

## AI and Society

This mini-workshop will explore the positive and negative impacts of Generative AI on society and its intersection with W3C's Ethical Web Principles and Vision. TPAC Attendees are encouraged to submit proposals for discussion topics such as sustainability, the open web, discoverability, inclusion, bias, neighborhoods, accessibility, epistemic injustice, UI, translations, threat modeling, and co-browsing experiences. 

## Age Assurance

Recent regulations restricting children's online access through age assurance requirements are driving major changes across the web, prompting a mini-workshop to continue discussions from last year's joint IAB/W3C workshop on age-based access restrictions. Attendees are encouraged to submit proposals for discussion topics such as privacy expectations, risk mapping, and the need for multi-stakeholder collaboration beyond purely technical solutions.

## Data Governance & Ownership

The World Wide Web has shifted from its original open, sharing-focused vision toward growing concerns over protecting vulnerable populations, managing copyright and AI data usage, and navigating centralized social media platforms that obscure data sharing and provenance. This workshop aims to explore new forms of data governance, data ownership, and decentralized alternatives. Attendees are encouraged to submit proposals for discussion topics such as user-centric alternatives, usage preferences, data provenance, and the role of web standards in balancing openness and ownership.
